# Key sounds

Every file here is cut from one recording:

- "Keyboard Typing, Medium Speed Typing, Fast Typing, Single Buttons, Enter Button, Space Button.wav"
- by ZvoolabAudio, https://freesound.org/people/ZvoolabAudio/sounds/456191/
- licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- a Genius membrane keyboard, recorded with a Rode NTG-2 and a Focusrite Forte

It was taken from Freesound's HQ preview (OGG, mono), because the original
needs a Freesound login. The recording has separate keystrokes of letter
keys (91-108 s), Enter (109-130 s) and the space bar (134-144 s). It has no
Backspace or Shift, so those use other Enter takes: on this keyboard they
are the same kind of long key, with a stabilizer bar.

Each take is split into the press (key going down) and the release (key
coming back up). Each clip was:

- high-passed at 80 Hz, to remove desk rumble
- cut to start 0.3 ms before its attack
- faded out over 10 ms once it decays 36 dB (press) or 32 dB (release)
  under its peak
- level-matched by the RMS of its first 25 ms: letters -24 dBFS, Enter,
  Backspace and Shift -22, the space bar -21, and each release 9 dB under
  its press
- saved as 16-bit mono 44.1 kHz WAV, imported uncompressed
